Call Diagonal Spread

A calendar with different strikes: the higher short leg adds a bullish lean to the time collected.

Open in the calculator →

Formula

SELL 1× CALL @ ATM+3 · BUY 1× CALL @ ATM+0 · exp+2

What you will learn

  • Risk: Limited
  • Reward: Limited
  • Max profit: Peaks at the short strike
  • Max loss: Net debit
  • When to use it: Like a calendar but directional: collecting time decay with a lean
  • Uses more than one expiration

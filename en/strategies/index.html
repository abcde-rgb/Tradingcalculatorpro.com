  • Ratio Call Write — Stock with two calls sold: one covered, one naked.

  • Stock Repair — On a losing stock position: one call bought and two sold higher, to lower the break-even without adding money.

  • Put Diagonal Spread — A put calendar with different strikes: the lower short leg adds a bearish lean.

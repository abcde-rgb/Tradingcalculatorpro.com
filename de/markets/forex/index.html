Forex

Foreign exchange is the largest market on earth: roughly $7.5 trillion changes hands every day. It has no central exchange — it is a network of banks (an OTC market) open 24 hours from Sunday evening to Friday evening. You always trade a PAIR: buying EUR/USD means buying euros and selling dollars in the same instant.

Wie es gemessen wird

PipThe standard minimum move: the 4th decimal (0.0001) on almost every pair. Exception: on yen (JPY) pairs the pip is the 2nd decimal (0.01).
PipetteOne tenth of a pip (5th decimal). Brokers quote pipettes to price the spread finely: 1.08542 is 1.0854 plus 2 pipettes.
Standard lot100,000 units of the base currency. Mini lot = 10,000. Micro lot = 1,000. Nano lot = 100.
Pip valueOne standard lot with USD as the quote currency: $10 per pip. Mini: $1. Micro: $0.10. If USD is not the quote currency you must convert.
NotionalLots × lot size × price. This is your real exposure — not what you deposit.
SpreadBid-ask difference measured in pips. It is your immediate cost of entry.
Swap / rolloverInterest paid or received for holding overnight (the rate differential between the two currencies). Wednesday is usually triple, covering the weekend settlement cycle.

Worked example: what you are actually risking

Account€5,000
Risk per trade1% = €50
PairEUR/USD at 1.0850
Stop loss25 pips
Pip value (1 standard lot)$10
Maximum size$50 ÷ (25 pips × $10) = 0.20 lots (2 mini lots)
Notional controlled0.20 × 100,000 = €20,000 (4× your account)

Look at the last line: a conservative 1% risk already has you moving four times your account. That is the point where leverage stops being an abstraction.

Häufige Fragen

What are the most traded currency pairs?

The "majors", all against the dollar: EUR/USD (by far the most liquid, ~28% of volume), USD/JPY, GBP/USD, USD/CHF, AUD/USD, USD/CAD and NZD/USD. Crosses without the dollar (EUR/GBP, EUR/JPY, GBP/JPY) carry less liquidity and wider spreads.

How much is one pip worth?

With one standard lot (100,000 units) and the dollar as quote currency, a pip is worth $10. One mini lot, $1. One micro lot, $0.10. If the quote currency is not the dollar you convert at the current rate.

When is the forex market open?

24 hours, five days a week: it opens Sunday 22:00 UTC (Sydney) and closes Friday 22:00 UTC (New York). The hours that matter are the overlaps: London-New York (12:00-16:00 UTC) concentrates the deepest liquidity.

Why is forex traded with so much leverage?

Because daily moves are small — a major pair travels 0.5-0.8% a day. Without leverage those moves would be irrelevant. That is also why it is dangerous: the same leverage that makes forex usable is what destroys accounts.

What is a forex swap and when is it charged?

It is the net interest for holding a position overnight, derived from the rate differential between the two currencies. It is applied at the New York close (22:00 UTC). Wednesday is charged triple because it covers weekend settlement.
